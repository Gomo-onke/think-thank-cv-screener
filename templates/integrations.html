No Zapier webhook = no emails. The portal hands each finished email to Zapier; your Zap (Gmail, Outlook or Email by Zapier) delivers it. "Sent" on this site means Zapier accepted it - open the Zap's run history to confirm delivery.

What your Zap receives

Every webhook has an event and a send_email field. Add a Zap Filter: send_email exactly matches yes, then a Send Email step using email_to, email_subject and email_body.

eventEmail goes to
application_receivedthe candidate (received, 14 business days)
recruiter_notificationthe recruitment lead (needs RECRUITMENT_LEAD_EMAIL)
human_escalationthe recruitment lead (a chat visitor asked for a person)
human_escalation_ackthe visitor (we passed your question to the team)
shortlist_emailthe candidate (congratulations, interview within 5 business days, Google Meet)
intern_welcome_emailthe intern (personal page link)
test_emailthe address typed in the test box
selection_emailthe candidate (stage set to Selected: congratulations and next steps)
rejection_emailthe candidate (stage set to Not selected)
application_rescreened, stage_changed, intern_placed, testno email (send_email = no) - data only
